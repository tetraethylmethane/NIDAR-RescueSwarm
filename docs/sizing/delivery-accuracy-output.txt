SECTION 5  REQUIREMENT DERIVATION
========================================================================================
  For an expected 14 pts/drop (Zone B value) in 3 m/s wind with 70%
  wind compensation, the geotag budget must be <= 1.63 m RSS.
  Case C (RTK + fusion + calibrated ground plane) gives 1.00 m
  total -> 17.7 pts/drop, 177 of 200.
  Case B (RTK, no fusion)  1.37 m -> 157 of 200.
  Case A (no RTK)          3.09 m -> 75 of 200.

  RTK is worth 82 points on delivery alone, before its effect on the 250-point
  detection-and-geotag score. Multi-frame fusion and ground-plane
  calibration add a further 20 points.

  PROPOSED REQUIREMENTS
    SYS-12  Geotag CEP50 <= 0.75 m with RTK (0.91 m RSS), verified
            against surveyed ground truth.
    SYS-15  >= 60% of drops within 2.0 m and >= 30% within 1.0 m of
            the survivor, over >= 30 drops in <= 3 m/s wind.
    SYS-31  Release gated at <= 0.30 m/s residual groundspeed.
    SYS-32  Wind-compensated release aim point, >= 70% of estimated
            drift removed.
