X8 coax 4x2x20"  k=0.85        5.45   16.34     775    6.72    12.0    1046     3  +28.8%
X8 coax 4x2x20"  k=0.80        5.45   16.34     819    6.72    11.4    1046     3  +36.3%
X8 coax 4x2x22"  k=0.80        5.66   16.98     791    5.77    11.8    1148     3  +31.5%

  k = coaxial interference factor on figure of merit. Published multirotor
  coaxial pairs sit around 0.80-0.87; 0.80/0.85 brackets the plausible range.
  /box = airframes that fit per row in the 12 ft (3.66 m) launch box.

  READING: coaxial costs ~30% hover power and buys NO footprint back,
  because stacking rotors does not shorten the arms. The X8 footprint is
  identical to the quad it replaces. Coaxial pays only when you are forced
  to shrink rotors to fit a box -- and the quad already fits 3 per row.

  Hex 6x18" is the one config that beats the quad on physics: 6 disks of
  18 in give 0.985 m2 against the quad's 0.811 m2, so power and disk loading
  both improve. Its cost is footprint, assembly and preflight time -- which
  land on setup, the only constraint with less than 20% margin.
