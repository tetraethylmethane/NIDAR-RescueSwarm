DOES OPTION D ACTUALLY WORK?  -  when is the first geotag needed
==============================================================================
  launch                          185 s
  RTK fixed                       290 s
  first sweep line begins         260 s (launch + 45 s queue + 20 s climb + 10 s transit)
  slack before first geotag       -30 s [INSUFFICIENT]

  Gate the first geotag on RTK-fixed rather than gating launch on it.
  If RTK has not fixed by the first detection, geotag in float and
  re-fuse once fixed -- the frame surplus makes that nearly free.

==============================================================================
WHY THE ABSOLUTE BASE POSITION BARELY MATTERS FOR DELIVERY
==============================================================================
  The base's absolute error shifts every aircraft position by the same
  vector. The survivor is geotagged from a drone carrying that shift,
  and the delivery drone flies to that coordinate carrying the SAME
  shift -- so the kit lands on the true survivor. The error cancels.

  It does NOT cancel if the 250-point geotag score is judged by
  comparing our DISPLAYED coordinates against surveyed truth. That is
  an open question and the reason to still want a good absolute fix.

  Consequence: a long survey-in buys accuracy we mostly do not need.
  Trading 90 s of survey-in for ~1-2 m of common-mode bias is a good
  trade when the window has 15 s of margin.
