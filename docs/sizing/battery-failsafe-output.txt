CURRENT DRAW AND OHMIC LOSS
==============================================================================
  Hover           :   42.3 A pack,  14.1 A/cell (31% of rating), I2R    54 W
  Peak (T/W 2.0)  :  114.8 A pack,  38.3 A/cell (85% of rating), I2R   395 W

  Hover I2R is 5.9% of hover power. The sizing model's flat
  Wh bucket does not account for it, so usable energy is optimistic by
  roughly that fraction.
==============================================================================
THE DEFECT: A THRESHOLD PICKED OFF THE WRONG CURVE
==============================================================================
  BATT_FS_VOLTSRC = 1 (sag-compensated), but BATT_RESISTANCE was unset,
  so it defaults to 0 and the compensation does nothing. The failsafe
  compares LOADED voltage against a threshold chosen from the RESTING
  curve.

  threshold       intended SoC    SoC it ACTUALLY fires at
  BATT_LOW_VOLT           20%                        53%
  BATT_CRT_VOLT           10%                        30%

  BATT_FS_LOW_ACT = 2 is RTL. Firing at 53% instead of 20 % aborts the
  search mid-mission and destroys the 2.0x reserve the pack was sized to.
==============================================================================
THE FIX  -  and the two options are ALTERNATIVES, not both
==============================================================================
  BATT_LOW_VOLT is compared against whatever BATT_FS_VOLTSRC selects.
  Setting the resistance AND lowering the threshold would apply the
  correction twice and push the failsafe dangerously LATE -- the
  opposite mistake, and a worse one.

  OPTION A  (recommended)  keep VOLTSRC = 1, set the resistance
      BATT_RESISTANCE = 0.040
      BATT_LOW_VOLT   = 20.4     unchanged -- correct once
      BATT_CRT_VOLT   = 19.2     compensation actually works
    ArduPilot reconstructs resting voltage, so the 3.40 / 3.20 V-per-cell
    thresholds finally mean what they were chosen to mean. This is the
    right option because the thresholds stay readable as SoC.

  OPTION B  raw voltage, thresholds moved onto the loaded curve
      BATT_FS_VOLTSRC = 0
      BATT_LOW_VOLT   = 18.48  (3.08 V/cell loaded at hover)
      BATT_CRT_VOLT   = 17.18  (2.86 V/cell loaded at hover)
    Simpler, no resistance to get wrong, but the thresholds are now tied
    to ONE current. They read early in a climb and late in a descent.

  THE REAL BACKSTOP is coulomb counting, and it is already correct:
      BATT_LOW_MAH = 2700  (20 %)      BATT_CRT_MAH = 1350  (10 %)
    It is independent of resistance, sag and temperature. ArduPilot acts
    on whichever failsafe fires first, so a correctly-set mAh threshold
    protects the pack even if the voltage estimate is wrong. Do NOT
    weaken it to compensate for a voltage threshold.

  ALSO SET, in either option:
      BATT_LOW_TIMER = 10   explicit, so a delivery manoeuvre transient
                            cannot end the mission by itself
==============================================================================
PEAK DRAW AT END OF MISSION  -  the brownout question
==============================================================================
  A delivery manoeuvre near the end of the search is the worst case:
  lowest SoC, highest transient current.

     SoC   temp      OCV   hover V   peak V  peak V/cell
    50%    25C    21.96     20.27    17.37         2.89
    50%    12C    21.96     19.78    16.04         2.67
    30%    25C    21.00     19.21    16.13         2.69
    30%    12C    21.00     18.68    14.70         2.45
    20%    25C    20.40     18.48    15.19         2.53
    20%    12C    20.40     17.91    13.63         2.27
    10%    25C    19.44     17.18    13.30         2.22
    10%    12C    19.44     16.47    11.38         1.90

  Worst case above: 13.63 V pack = 2.27 V/cell.

  MODEL VALIDITY. This is a purely ohmic sag model, so it OVERSTATES the
  droop at the bottom-right of the table -- real cells add polarisation
  but the ESCs also current-limit long before 1.9 V/cell. Read the 10 %
  rows as 'do not go there', not as a prediction. You should never be
  there anyway: with the failsafe fixed, the aircraft lands at 20 %.
  The 5 V avionics rail is fed from this bus. A BEC needs roughly 1-2 V
  of headroom, so the rail is safe on voltage -- but the Jetson, the FC,
  the mesh radio and the camera all sit behind it, and a companion
  brownout loses perception AND the mission-state feed to the GCS.
  Specify the BEC for the WORST case above, not the nominal 21.6 V.

==============================================================================
WHAT IS PREDICTED VERSUS MEASURED
==============================================================================
  EVERY number above is a prediction from datasheet-class cell data.
  None of it is measured. Before first flight:

    * Bench-discharge the real pack with a current step and compute
      R = dV / dI. That measurement beats this model outright and takes
      an afternoon.
    * Log a hover and compare measured sag against the table above.
    * Only then trust BATT_LOW_VOLT.

  The values here exist because the alternative currently in the
  firmware is BATT_RESISTANCE = 0, which is not a better estimate.

  If you have a MATLAB licence, a Simscape Electrical pack model adds
  diffusion/relaxation dynamics, per-cell imbalance and a real thermal
  network. Those refine the numbers; they do not change the finding.
