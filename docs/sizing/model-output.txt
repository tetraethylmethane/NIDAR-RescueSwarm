  Margin against the 300 s rule: 15 s (5%)

  The single biggest lever is RTK convergence and companion boot. Mitigations:
   1. Survey and start the RTK base BEFORE the window opens (it is ground equipment).
   2. Pre-load the TensorRT engine into a warm cache; do not JIT-build at boot.
   3. Cache the GNSS almanac (hot start) - saves 30-40 s over a true cold start.
   4. Ask the organisers whether power-on may precede the window (question 6 in the plan).
   5. Measure all of these on real hardware in Phase 5. These are estimates, not data.
