====================================================================================
THE BOM IS RIGHT AND THE MODEL IS OPTIMISTIC
====================================================================================
  Read the 'x mis' column again. Against the reserve policy the whole
  design was sized to -- hover endurance >= 2.0x mission time:

     20 in / 6S2P  (design point)   1.78x   FAILS THE POLICY
     18 in / 6S2P                   1.65x   FAILS
     18 in / 6S3P  (the BOM)        2.20x   passes
     20 in / 6S3P                   2.38x   passes

  With generic component masses the 6S2P pack scrapes 2.01x. With real
  Indian parts it does not close. THE BOM'S 6S3P PACK IS NOT A STALE
  LEFTOVER -- IT IS THE CORRECT RESPONSE TO HEAVIER INDIAN COMPONENTS.
  Whoever built the BOM did the engineering the model never had.

  The consequence is that the design point, not the BOM, is what needs
  to move. The 5.93 kg / 17.8 kg figures once in the README were the
  Indian-BOM aircraft all along.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IS 6S3P AFFORDABLE?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  18 in, 6S3P  DESIGN    fleet 18.70 kg, margin 25 %, hover 15.8 min (2.05x)
  20 in, 6S3P            fleet 18.91 kg, margin 24 %, hover 17.1 min (2.23x)

  Yes. Both keep close to 30 % margin against the 25 kg cap. The
  reserve policy is self-imposed rather than a rule, but it exists so
  that a re-sweep and a 4 min loiter stay available after the nominal
  mission. Relaxing it to justify a lighter pack would be moving the
  goalposts to fit the hardware.
