{\LARGE\bfseries Campus asset checklist}\\[1.5mm]
{\color{dim}Project RescueSwarm · NIDAR 2026--27 · what to confirm before
submitting the funding request}\\[1mm]
{\color{rule}\rule{\linewidth}{1.2pt}}\\[2.5mm]

\textbf{Why.} Find out what the institute already owns \emph{before} asking it
for money. Every confirmed \emph{yes} either reduces the request or removes a
risk, and none of it costs the project any capability --- it is the same
equipment, borrowed instead of bought.

\vspace{1.5mm}
\textbf{How.} Grouped by \emph{where you go}, so each visit is one
conversation. \textbf{Ask the question as written.} The general question invites
a misleading answer: \emph{``do you have a GPS?''} gets a yes that turns out to
be a total station. Record the answer, not just yes/no --- ``yes, but booked
until March'' is a no.

\bigsec{A · Equipment that would leave the budget --- \rs{70,500}}

\where{$\rightarrow$ Civil Engineering, survey laboratory}

\entry{RTK base receiver}{\rs{18,000} · biggest single item, least likely to just work}
\ask{``Do you have a survey-grade GNSS receiver that can run as a \textbf{base
station} and output \textbf{RTCM3 corrections} over serial or IP to a
third-party rover, at about 1\,Hz? Would it be free for roughly eight field
days?''}
\nope{it is a total station (not GNSS); it only post-processes and cannot
stream live corrections; it is booked for coursework in our window.}
\ansline

\entry{Survey tripod + tribrach}{\rs{4,000}}
\ask{``A standard tripod with a 5/8-inch thread and a tribrach we could borrow
for field days?''}
\ansline

\where{$\rightarrow$ Robotics / aeromodelling club}

\entry{Safety-pilot transmitter}{\rs{8,000}}
\ask{``Do you have an \textbf{ExpressLRS} transmitter, or a radio with a
\textbf{module bay} that can take an ELRS module?''}
\nope{it is FlySky, Spektrum or any non-ELRS set --- wrong protocol, cannot
bind to our receivers. A module bay alone is enough; the module is cheap.}
\ansline

\entry{Battery chargers}{\rs{14,000}}
\ask{``A charger that balance-charges \textbf{6S lithium at 5\,A or more}, and
measures \textbf{per-cell internal resistance}?''}
\nope{it cannot measure internal resistance --- that capability is why the
separate cell tester was already deleted from the budget.}
\ansline

\where{$\rightarrow$ Mechanical Engineering laboratory}

\entry{Calibrated scale}{\rs{12,000}}
\ask{``A calibrated balance reading to about 1\,g up to 10\,kg --- and
\textbf{can it leave the building} for a competition day?''}
\nope{it is bench-bound. This decides the Rule C2 weigh-in, so it has to
travel. If it cannot, we still buy one.}
\ansline

\entry{Load cell and DAQ}{would offset the \rs{8,000} thrust stand}
\ask{``Do you have a \textbf{load cell up to about 10\,kg with a data-logger or
DAQ} we could use for propeller thrust measurement?''}
{\small This one is \textbf{load-bearing for the project}: our motors publish no
thrust curve, so this measurement decides whether the aircraft hovers.}\\[1.2mm]
\ansline

\where{$\rightarrow$ Departmental stores}

\entry{Equipment cases}{\rs{9,000} · foam-lined, must survive transport to the venue}
\ansline
\entry{Charger PSU}{\rs{1,500} · 12\,V at 20\,A or better, usable in the field}
\ansline

\where{$\rightarrow$ Institute safety office}

\entry{Fire extinguisher, sand, lithium charging bags}{\rs{4,000}}
\ask{``A \textbf{CO$_2$ or Class D} extinguisher and lithium charging bags that
can be \textbf{signed out to a field site}?''}
{\small 54 lithium cells are cycled across this programme. Thermal runaway is a
real failure mode, so this capability is kept whether or not it is
borrowed.}\\[1.2mm]
\ansline

\bigsec{B · Stock and consumables --- up to about \rs{15,000}}

{\small Drawn from the \rs{13,000} fasteners line and the \rs{21,400} stock
line. Partial answers still help.}\\[2mm]

\entry{3D printer filament}{the institute already provides the printer}
\ask{``Does the department supply \textbf{PETG or ASA filament}, or do we buy

\ansline

\entry{Carbon-fibre offcuts}{machine shop}
\ask{``Do you hold \textbf{CF plate or tube stock}, or offcuts from previous

\ansline

\entry{Fasteners}{stores}
\ask{``Is there a stock of \textbf{M3 socket-cap, nyloc and washers} we can draw

\bigsec{C · Questions that remove a risk rather than a cost}

\where{$\rightarrow$ Dean R\&D / accounts office}

\entry{Insurance}{resolves a \rs{25,000} open item \emph{and} a flight blocker}
\ask{``Does the institute hold \textbf{third-party liability cover that extends
to student projects operating unmanned aircraft}? If not, is it required before
we fly?''}
{\small\textcolor{warn}{This is the single unresolved item in the budget.}
Cover is commonly mandatory for unmanned operations in India. If the institute
already holds a policy that extends to us, the \rs{25,000} stays out and the
flight blocker clears. If it does not, we must know \textbf{before any flight},
not after.}\\[1.2mm]
\ansline

\entry{Travel and participation fund}{money that is not in this budget at all}
\ask{``Is there a \textbf{separate fund for competition travel, accommodation
and entry fees}, distinct from equipment funding?''}
{\small Our request covers \textbf{equipment only}. Getting three aircraft and a
team to the venue is costed nowhere. Many institutes fund this from a different
head, so it need not compete with the equipment request.}\\[1.2mm]
\ansline

\entry{Sanction thresholds and budget heads}{}
\ask{``What are the \textbf{sanction limits}, and which \textbf{head} should
capital equipment versus consumables be indented under?''}
{\small We need this to submit correctly, not to structure around it. Our
tranches are tied to technical milestones and must not be presented as a split
indent.}\\[1.2mm]
\ansline

\entry{Flight-testing site}{}
\ask{``Is there an approved \textbf{open area on or near campus} we may use for
UAV flight testing, and what permission is needed?''}
\ansline

\bigsec{After the walk-around}

\begin{tabular}{@{}r@{\hspace{2.5mm}}p{158mm}@{}}
1. & Total the confirmed items in section A.\\
2. & Regenerate the workbook with those lines zeroed, then re-check the covering
     note against tab 00 --- the ask, the capital share and the institutional
     contribution all move together.\\
3. & If anything in \textbf{D} came back unavailable, add it back to the request
     \emph{before} submitting.\\
4. & Open the covering note with what you found. ``We audited the institute's
     existing assets and reduced this request by \rs{X} before submitting it''
     is a materially stronger opening than the same number without it.\\
