# Campus asset checklist

**Purpose.** Find out what the institute already owns, before asking it for
money. Every confirmed *yes* either reduces the request or removes a risk, and
none of it costs the project any capability — it is the same equipment,
borrowed instead of bought.

**How to use it.** Grouped by *where you go*, not by budget line, so each visit
is one conversation. Ask the question as written. The general question invites a
misleading answer: *"do you have a GPS?"* gets a yes that turns out to be a
total station.

**Record the answer, not just yes/no.** "Yes, but booked until March" is a no.
"Yes, but it can't leave the building" is a no for the weigh-in scale.

## After the walk-around

1. Total the confirmed items in section A.
2. Regenerate the workbook with those lines zeroed, and re-check the covering
   note against tab 00 — the ask, the capital share and the institutional
   contribution all move together.
3. If anything in **D** came back unavailable, add it back to the request before
   submitting.
4. Open the covering note with what you found. *"We audited the institute's
   existing assets and reduced this request by ₹X before submitting it"* is a
   materially stronger opening than the same number without it.
